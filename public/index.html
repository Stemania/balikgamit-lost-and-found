      Welcome to BalikGamit
      
              
      BalikGamit ("return the item") is the campus Lost & Found board. Anyone who loses or finds something on campus can post it here, so the item can find its way back to its owner. Reports are public, searchable, and tracked from the moment they are posted until the item is returned.
      
            
      
          
      

          
      
            
      How to use it
      5 steps
      
            
        
              
      1. 
                1
                
        
                  
        Report a lost or found item
        
                  
        Click Report item at the top right. Choose I lost something or I found something, then fill in the item name, category, date, location and how to reach you, and click Submit report. Fields marked * are required; the form tells you exactly what is missing.
        
                
        
                
              
        2. 
              
      2. 
                2
                
        
                  
        Browse and search the board
        
                  
        Use the sidebar to open All open, Lost items or Found items. Narrow the list with the category and status filters, or press / and type a name, place or category to search.
        
                
        
                Browse found items
              
        3. 
              
      3. 
                3
                
        
                  
        Open an item's details
        
                  
        Click any row to open its detail panel. It shows the description, where and when the item was lost or found, who reported it and how to contact them, plus a timeline of the report.
        
                
        
                Open the list
              
        4. 
              
      4. 
                4
                
        
                  
        Mark it as claimed when it's returned
        
                  
        Once the owner has the item back, open its detail panel, click Mark as claimed and confirm. The report moves to Claimed and counts toward the return rate on the dashboard.
        
                
        
                View claimed

      What the tags mean
      
              
      
                
      Lost
      Someone lost this item and is looking for it.
      
                
      Found
      Someone found this item and is waiting for the owner.
      
                
      Open
      The report is active; the item has not been returned yet.
      
                
      Claimed
      The item is back with its owner; the report is closed.
      
              
      
            
      
            
      
              
      Good to know
      
              
        
                
      • Be specific. Color, brand, stickers or what's inside help the owner recognize the item.
        • 
                
      • Protect your privacy. Don't post phone numbers or home addresses; use a Messenger name or school email.
        • 
                
      • Claim only when returned. Mark an item as claimed after the owner actually has it back.
        • 
                
      • First visit can be slow. The free hosting sleeps after about 15 minutes idle, so the first load may take up to a minute.

      About this project
      IT 412 Platform Technologies · Group 4
      
            
      
              
      BalikGamit was built as our final project for IT 412, a cloud web application deployed on a Platform as a Service. We chose a Lost & Found system because:
      
              
        
                
      • It solves a real campus problem. Lost items usually end up scattered across offices, group chats and social media posts; one searchable board makes them easier to find.
        • 
                
      • It naturally needs everything the project asks for. Users submit reports (user input), the server validates them and handles claiming (business logic), and every report is stored in a cloud database and shown back on the site.
        • 
                
      • It maps cleanly to a REST API. GET /api/items lists reports, POST /api/items creates one, and PATCH /api/items/:id/claim closes one, with proper 200, 201 and 400 responses.
        • 
                
      • It fits the free cloud tiers. It runs on Render (PaaS) and MongoDB Atlas at no cost, which shows how PaaS lets a small team focus on the app instead of the servers.
